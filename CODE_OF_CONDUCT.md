# Code of Conduct

We aim to make participation in Qiskit Learning Lab a respectful, constructive experience. Be considerate, welcome questions, critique ideas rather than people, and avoid harassment or discrimination. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation that violates these expectations.

## Reporting

To report a concern privately, contact the maintainer through the contact details on the GitHub profile at <https://github.com/egkhor>, or open a private report at <https://github.com/egkhor/qiskit-learning-lab/security/advisories/new>. Please do not post reports of conduct problems in public issues.
